## Prezent (Present Tenses)

### Present Simple

| Formă | Utilizare | Exemple Afirmative | Exemple Negative | Exemple Interogative |
|-------|-----------|--------------------|-----------------|-----------------------|
| Verb (forma de bază) / Verb + -s (pers. III sing.) | Acțiuni obișnuite, adevăruri generale | I **eat** breakfast every day.<br>She **eats** breakfast every day.<br>We **eat** breakfast every day.<br>They **eat** breakfast every day. | I **don't eat** breakfast.<br>She **doesn't eat** breakfast.<br>We **don't eat** breakfast.<br>They **don't eat** breakfast. | **Do** you eat breakfast?<br>**Does** she eat breakfast?<br>**Do** we eat breakfast?<br>**Do** they eat breakfast? |

### Present Continuous

| Formă | Utilizare | Exemple Afirmative | Exemple Negative | Exemple Interogative |
|-------|-----------|--------------------|-----------------|-----------------------|
| am/is/are + verb + -ing | Acțiuni în desfășurare, acțiuni temporare | I **am eating** now.<br>She **is eating** now.<br>We **are eating** now.<br>They **are eating** now. | I **'m not eating** now.<br>She **isn't eating** now.<br>We **aren't eating** now.<br>They **aren't eating** now. | **Am** I eating now?<br>**Is** she eating now?<br>**Are** we eating now?<br>**Are** they eating now? |

### Present Perfect

| Formă | Utilizare | Exemple Afirmative | Exemple Negative | Exemple Interogative |
|-------|-----------|--------------------|-----------------|-----------------------|
| have/has + participiul trecut | Acțiuni începute în trecut și continuate în prezent | I **have eaten** breakfast.<br>She **has eaten** breakfast.<br>We **have eaten** breakfast.<br>They **have eaten** breakfast. | I **haven't eaten** breakfast.<br>She **hasn't eaten** breakfast.<br>We **haven't eaten** breakfast.<br>They **haven't eaten** breakfast. | **Have** I eaten?<br>**Has** she eaten?<br>**Have** we eaten?<br>**Have** they eaten? |

### Present Perfect Continuous

| Formă | Utilizare | Exemple Afirmative | Exemple Negative | Exemple Interogative |
|-------|-----------|--------------------|-----------------|-----------------------|
| have/has + been + verb + -ing | Acțiuni începute în trecut, cu accent pe durată | I **have been eating** all morning.<br>She **has been eating** all morning.<br>We **have been eating** all morning.<br>They **have been eating** all morning. | I **haven't been eating** all morning.<br>She **hasn't been eating** all morning.<br>We **haven't been eating** all morning.<br>They **haven't been eating** all morning. | **Have** I **been eating**?<br>**Has** she **been eating**?<br>**Have** we **been eating**?<br>**Have** they **been eating**? |

## Trecut (Past Tenses)

### Past Simple

| Formă | Utilizare | Exemple Afirmative | Exemple Negative | Exemple Interogative |
|-------|-----------|--------------------|-----------------|-----------------------|
| Verb (forma a II-a) | Acțiuni finalizate în trecut | I **ate** breakfast yesterday.<br>She **ate** breakfast yesterday.<br>We **ate** breakfast yesterday.<br>They **ate** breakfast yesterday. | I **didn't eat** breakfast yesterday.<br>She **didn't eat** breakfast yesterday.<br>We **didn't eat** breakfast yesterday.<br>They **didn't eat** breakfast yesterday. | **Did** I eat breakfast?<br>**Did** she eat breakfast?<br>**Did** we eat breakfast?<br>**Did** they eat breakfast? |

### Past Continuous

| Formă | Utilizare | Exemple Afirmative | Exemple Negative | Exemple Interogative |
|-------|-----------|--------------------|-----------------|-----------------------|
| was/were + verb + -ing | Acțiuni în desfășurare în trecut | I **was eating** when you called.<br>She **was eating** when you called.<br>We **were eating** when you called.<br>They **were eating** when you called. | I **wasn't eating** when you called.<br>She **wasn't eating** when you called.<br>We **weren't eating** when you called.<br>They **weren't eating** when you called. | **Was** I eating then?<br>**Was** she eating then?<br>**Were** we eating then?<br>**Were** they eating then? |

### Past Perfect

| Formă | Utilizare | Exemple Afirmative | Exemple Negative | Exemple Interogative |
|-------|-----------|--------------------|-----------------|-----------------------|
| had + participiul trecut | Acțiuni finalizate înainte de alt moment din trecut | I **had eaten** before I left.<br>She **had eaten** before she left.<br>We **had eaten** before we left.<br>They **had eaten** before they left. | I **hadn't eaten** before I left.<br>She **hadn't eaten** before she left.<br>We **hadn't eaten** before we left.<br>They **hadn't eaten** before they left. | **Had** I eaten before?<br>**Had** she eaten before?<br>**Had** we eaten before?<br>**Had** they eaten before? |

### Past Perfect Continuous

| Formă | Utilizare | Exemple Afirmative | Exemple Negative | Exemple Interogative |
|-------|-----------|--------------------|-----------------|-----------------------|
| had + been + verb + -ing | Acțiuni care au durat până la un moment din trecut | I **had been eating** for an hour.<br>She **had been eating** for an hour.<br>We **had been eating** for an hour.<br>They **had been eating** for an hour. | I **hadn't been eating** for an hour.<br>She **hadn't been eating** for an hour.<br>We **hadn't been eating** for an hour.<br>They **hadn't been eating** for an hour. | **Had** I **been eating** long?<br>**Had** she **been eating** long?<br>**Had** we **been eating** long?<br>**Had** they **been eating** long? |
